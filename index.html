What it covers

Neighborhood workshops
The first public step for a major site plan, PD amendment, plat, or rezoning, usually months before a vote. Address, parcels, applicant, and what is proposed, from the notice PDF.
Board agendas
City Plan Board, Development Review Board, Board of Adjustment, Historic Preservation Board, and the City Commission's land-use items, with petition numbers and the staff report.
Building permits
Not yet. The city stopped publishing its permit feed in 2023. A records request is in, and permits join the ledger when the city provides an export.

Who it is for

Trade contractors who want to know about a 200-unit site plan before the general contractor starts calling. Builders and developers tracking what is coming. Commercial and land brokers. Land-use attorneys, civil engineers, and title companies who need the docket without reading four city websites.

It is not news. The Chronicle and Mainstreet cover the votes on the big projects. Most of what is in the ledger never gets a story, and the rest shows up here months earlier.

How it is made

  • Sources are the City of Gainesville's neighborhood workshop notices and its eScribe agenda portal. Nothing is scraped from the permitting portal.
  • A script pulls every Sunday night. A person reads every row against its source before anything is sent. A row that cannot be verified is left out, not guessed.
  • Errors are corrected in the next issue and noted. Reply to any issue to report one.

GNV Ledger lists public records for the City of Gainesville, Florida. It is not affiliated with the City. Every row links to the record it came from; if a row is wrong, reply to any issue or write to austin@augmentedinfosystems.com and it gets fixed and noted.
